Source & scope

Basic fee: WIPO Schedule of Fees — CHF 653 (black-and-white) / CHF 903 (colour) for an application; CHF 653 for renewal. China individual fees: WIPO Information Notice No. 9/2026 (effective 12 April 2026) — application/subsequent designation CHF 220 first class + CHF 110 each additional; renewal CHF 441 first class + CHF 220 each additional; collective/certification application CHF 661 / CHF 331. See wipo.int Schedule of Fees and Notice 9/2026 (PDF). China declares an individual fee, so the CHF 100 complementary fee does not apply to China. This estimate covers a single designation of China; other designated members, your home-office fee, and any agent service fee are not included. CHF→USD shown for planning only at ≈1.15.
