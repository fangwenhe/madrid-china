Central attack survived? Transform the China designation within 3 months

If your basic application or registration falls within the first five years, the International Bureau cancels the international registration — including the China designation. CNIPA lets you transform (变通) it into a national application within 3 months from the cancellation date, and your new Chinese application keeps the original international registration date. CNIPA's conditions for a transformation (all must be met):
  • The international registration was valid in China at the time of cancellation.
  • It was cancelled under Article 6quater of the Madrid Protocol — i.e. the basic mark at your office of origin ceased to have effect.
  • The national application is filed within 3 months of the cancellation date.
  • The mark details match the international registration: IR number, IR date, date of any subsequent designation, the reproduction, the mark name, and the applicant's name and address.
  • The goods/services do not exceed either the scope of protection of the IR or the scope of the cancellation — you can drop items, never add them.
How to file: through a CNIPA-recorded Chinese agent (a foreign attorney cannot file directly), with (1) the transformation request, (2) a power of attorney, (3) a copy of the cancellation notice, and (4) a national application package meeting normal CNIPA requirements. A multi-class IR transformed into separate per-class applications needs a separate transformation package for each class. Official fees follow the national application standard, not the Madrid fee schedule.
